# ⚔️ StudyBuddy — Level Up Your Learning
## 🧠 Transform Studying Into a Progression System

**StudyBuddy — Level Up Your Learning** is an AI-powered gamified study companion designed to make learning more engaging, structured, and measurable.

Instead of treating studying as a simple checklist, StudyBuddy transforms real learning activity into a progression system where students can complete quests, challenge dungeon bosses, earn XP, improve their attributes, unlock skills, track mastery, and receive AI-powered guidance.

> **Study. Progress. Level Up.**
> ## ⭐ Key Features

- 🤖 **AI System Guide** — Personalized study assistance, explanations, planning, and tactical guidance.
- 📚 **Personal Syllabus** — Organize subjects, units, and topics around your actual curriculum.
- ⚔️ **Dungeon Battles** — Test knowledge through interactive quiz-based dungeon challenges.
- 👹 **Boss Challenges** — Face progressively difficult original boss encounters.
- ✨ **XP & Level System** — Earn experience from real study activity and progress through levels.
- 🕸️ **Progression Web** — Visualize subject mastery, topic growth, and learning attributes.
- 🌌 **3D Progression** — Explore your learning progression through an interactive 3D visualization.
- 🧬 **Character & Power** — Develop attributes such as Intelligence, Strength, Vitality, Agility, and Sense.
- 🌳 **Skill Tree** — Unlock progression abilities based on your learning journey.
- 📖 **Smart Revision** — Identify weak areas and prioritize revision intelligently.
- 🎯 **Focus Training** — Track focused study sessions and build consistency.
- 🏆 **Achievements** — Earn achievements for meaningful learning milestones.
- 📊 **Learning Analytics** — Monitor mastery, activity, progress, and study patterns.
- 🗓️ **Progress Calendar** — Visualize your study consistency and activity.
- 🎮 **Quests & Missions** — Turn study goals into structured missions.
- 📑 **PDF Study Tools** — Work with study material and convert supported documents.
- 🎙️ **Voice Study Mode** — Interact with the study system using voice capabilities where supported.
- 👥 **Study Party / Guild Features** — Optional collaborative study experiences.

## 🛡️ Design Philosophy

StudyBuddy is built around several principles:

- **Real Progression** — Progression should come from meaningful learning activity.
- **Personalization** — Study recommendations should adapt to the learner.
- **Measurable Mastery** — Track topic-level understanding rather than relying only on XP.
- **Responsible Gamification** — Game mechanics should support learning rather than encourage unhealthy excessive study.
- **Original Visual Identity** — The interface uses an original dark-fantasy/futuristic aesthetic without relying on copyrighted anime characters or artwork.
- **Privacy & Security** — API keys and sensitive credentials should remain server-side and should never be committed to the repository.

## 📈 Project Vision
